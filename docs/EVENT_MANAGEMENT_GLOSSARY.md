# 1. Event Management Terminology

## Event

A planned gathering or experience organized for a specific purpose,
audience, or objective.

Examples include conferences, festivals, workshops, exhibitions,
meetups, fundraisers, and corporate events.

## Event Management

The process of planning, organizing, executing, and evaluating an event.

Event management may include:

- Planning
- Budgeting
- Marketing
- Registration
- Venue management
- Vendor coordination
- Staffing
- Security
- Technology
- Post-event evaluation

## Event Organizer

A person, organization, or team responsible for planning and executing
an event.

## Event Manager

A professional responsible for coordinating event activities and
ensuring that the event meets its objectives.

## Event Coordinator

A person who manages specific operational tasks before and during an
event.

## Event Team

The group of people responsible for planning and delivering an event.

## Event Objective

A measurable or clearly defined outcome that an event is intended to
achieve.

Examples:

- Generate leads
- Educate attendees
- Raise funds
- Build community
- Launch a product

## Event Scope

The defined boundaries of an event.

Scope may include:

- Audience
- Location
- Duration
- Activities
- Budget
- Deliverables

## Event Lifecycle

The complete sequence of activities involved in an event.

Typical stages include:

1. Concept
2. Planning
3. Preparation
4. Execution
5. Evaluation
6. Follow-up

## Event Brief

A document summarizing the key requirements and objectives of an event.

## Event Proposal

A document describing a proposed event, including objectives, audience,
activities, budget, and expected outcomes.

## Event Theme

The central concept or creative direction of an event.

## Event Format

The structure used to deliver an event.

Examples:

- In-person
- Virtual
- Hybrid
- Workshop
- Conference
- Festival

## Event Program

The planned sequence of activities, sessions, presentations, and other
event elements.

## Event Agenda

A schedule showing when event activities take place.

## Run of Show

A detailed operational timeline used by event staff to coordinate
activities during the event.

# 2. Event Planning Terms

## Pre-Event Planning

All activities completed before the event begins.

Examples:

- Venue booking
- Marketing
- Registration
- Staffing
- Vendor coordination
- Technical preparation

## Event Timeline

A chronological list of important event tasks and deadlines.

## Milestone

A significant point in the event planning timeline.

## Deliverable

A specific item or result that must be completed.

## Workback Schedule

A planning schedule that starts with the event date and works backward
to establish deadlines.

## Checklist

A list of tasks used to track event preparation.

## Contingency Plan

A predefined response for potential problems or unexpected situations.

## Risk Assessment

The process of identifying and evaluating potential event risks.

## Risk Register

A document listing identified risks, their likelihood, impact, and
mitigation strategies.

## Stakeholder

A person or organization affected by or involved in the event.

## Event Owner

The person or organization ultimately responsible for the event.

## Project Manager

A person responsible for managing the overall project plan,
resources, deadlines, and deliverables.

## Planning Committee

A group responsible for making event planning decisions.

## Event Objective

A specific result the event intends to achieve.

## Key Performance Indicator

A measurable value used to determine whether an event achieved its
objectives.

## Success Metric

A measurement used to evaluate event performance.

# 3. Event Operations Terms

## Event Operations

The practical activities required to deliver an event successfully.

## Operations Team

Staff responsible for event logistics and execution.

## Front of House

Areas and activities visible to attendees.

Examples:

- Registration desk
- Lobby
- Seating
- Information desk

## Back of House

Operational areas not normally visible to attendees.

Examples:

- Staff rooms
- Storage
- Production areas
- Equipment rooms

## Event Command Center

A central location where event staff coordinate operations.

## Operations Manual

A document containing operational procedures and instructions.

## Standard Operating Procedure

A documented process for performing a recurring task.

## Staff Briefing

A meeting where staff receive event instructions.

## Shift

A defined period during which a staff member works.

## Staff Schedule

A timetable showing when staff members are assigned to work.

## Escalation

The process of transferring an issue to someone with greater authority
or expertise.

## Incident

An unexpected event requiring attention or intervention.

## Incident Log

A record of incidents occurring during an event.

## Service Desk

A location where attendees can receive assistance.

## Information Desk

A designated location for attendee questions and event information.

# 4. Venue Terms

## Venue

The physical or virtual location where an event takes place.

## Venue Capacity

The maximum number of people permitted in a venue or room.

## Venue Walkthrough

A physical inspection of a venue before an event.

## Floor Plan

A diagram showing the arrangement of spaces and equipment.

## Room Layout

The arrangement of seating, tables, stages, and other elements.

## Theater Style

Rows of chairs facing a stage or presentation area.

## Classroom Style

Tables and chairs arranged facing a presentation area.

## Boardroom Style

A large table surrounded by chairs.

## U-Shape

Tables arranged in a U formation.

## Banquet Style

Round tables arranged for meals or group interaction.

## Cabaret Style

Tables arranged to face a presentation area while leaving space for
interaction.

## Breakout Room

A smaller room used for parallel sessions or activities.

## Main Stage

The primary presentation or performance area.

## Green Room

A private area used by speakers, performers, or presenters before an
appearance.

## Loading Dock

A location where event equipment and materials are loaded or unloaded.

## Venue Access

The ability of organizers, vendors, and attendees to enter the venue.

## Setup Period

The time available to prepare the venue before attendees arrive.

## Teardown

The process of removing equipment and restoring the venue after the
event.

## Venue Contract

A legal agreement between the event organizer and venue.

## Venue Walkthrough Checklist

A list used to verify venue requirements before the event.

# 5. Sponsorship Terms

## Sponsor

An organization or individual providing financial or other support to an
event.

## Sponsorship

A partnership in which a sponsor provides resources in exchange for
agreed benefits.

## Sponsorship Package

A predefined set of benefits offered to sponsors.

## Sponsorship Tier

A sponsorship level with a specific price and benefits.

Common examples:

- Platinum
- Gold
- Silver
- Bronze

## Title Sponsor

The sponsor receiving prominent naming or branding rights.

## Presenting Sponsor

A major sponsor associated prominently with the event.

## Supporting Sponsor

A sponsor providing financial or in-kind support at a lower tier.

## In-Kind Sponsor

A sponsor providing products or services instead of money.

## Sponsorship Prospect

A potential sponsor.

## Sponsorship Lead

A potential sponsor who has been identified or contacted.

## Sponsorship Outreach

The process of contacting potential sponsors.

## Sponsorship Proposal

A document explaining the event and proposed sponsorship opportunity.

## Sponsorship Agreement

A formal agreement defining sponsor responsibilities and benefits.

## Sponsorship Activation

Activities used to deliver sponsor benefits and engage attendees.

## Brand Activation

A marketing activity designed to create interaction between attendees
and a sponsor brand.

## Sponsor Booth

A designated area where a sponsor presents its organization or
products.

## Sponsor Benefits

The promotional or experiential benefits provided to sponsors.

Examples:

- Logo placement
- Speaking opportunities
- Booth space
- Social media promotion
- Website placement

## Sponsor Recognition

Acknowledgment of sponsors through event communications and materials.

## Sponsor ROI

Return on investment generated from sponsorship participation.

# 6. Marketing Terms

## Event Marketing

Activities used to attract attendees and promote an event.

## Marketing Campaign

A coordinated set of promotional activities.

## Target Audience

The specific group an event is designed to reach.

## Audience Persona

A fictional representation of a typical target attendee.

## Event Branding

The visual and verbal identity of an event.

## Brand Guidelines

Rules defining how event branding should be used.

## Campaign Strategy

The overall approach used to promote an event.

## Marketing Channel

A platform or method used to communicate with potential attendees.

Examples:

- Email
- Social media
- Search
- Website
- Advertising

## Email Marketing

Promoting an event through email communications.

## Social Media Marketing

Using social platforms to promote an event.

## Content Marketing

Using useful or informative content to attract an audience.

## Paid Advertising

Promotional activity where an organization pays for exposure.

## Organic Reach

Audience exposure achieved without direct advertising payment.

## Call to Action

A message encouraging an audience to perform a specific action.

Examples:

- Register now
- Buy tickets
- Submit a proposal

## Landing Page

A webpage designed to encourage a specific action.

## Conversion

A desired action completed by a visitor.

## Conversion Rate

The percentage of visitors who complete a desired action.

## Click-Through Rate

The percentage of users who click a link after seeing it.

## Engagement Rate

A measurement of audience interaction with content.

## Campaign Reach

The number of people exposed to a campaign.

## Campaign Impressions

The number of times promotional content is displayed.

## UTM Parameter

A URL parameter used to track marketing traffic sources.

## Regular Registration

The standard registration period.

## Late Registration

Registration completed close to or after the standard deadline.

## Waitlist

A list of people waiting for available event spaces.

## Check-In

The process of confirming an attendee's arrival.

## Badge

An identification card worn by an attendee.

## Badge Printing

The process of producing attendee identification badges.

## QR Code Check-In

## Registration Desk

The location where attendees register or receive assistance.

## No-Show

## Cancellation

The process of withdrawing an event registration.

## Refund

Money returned to an attendee after an eligible cancellation.

## Plenary Session

A structured discussion involving participants around a shared topic.

## Networking Session

## Expo

## Exhibition Hall

## Poster Session

An invitation for speakers to submit presentation ideas.

# Glossary Usage Guidelines

This glossary is intended as a general reference.

Event terminology may vary between organizations, industries, countries,
and event formats.

Organizers should define specialized terminology when introducing it
to participants or staff.

When creating event documentation:

- Use terms consistently.
- Define uncommon abbreviations.
- Avoid unnecessary jargon.
- Explain technical terms when communicating with general audiences.
- Use the terminology relevant to the event format.
- Keep attendee-facing language simple.
- Maintain consistent terminology across registration, marketing, and
  operational documents.
